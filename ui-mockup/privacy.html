Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Raven Project TRP Router playground (the “Site”) handles information when you use our Internet Computer–hosted application. We do not operate a traditional account system with email and password; access uses Internet Identity only.

What we collect

  • Internet Identity principal. When you sign in, your browser talks to Internet Identity and our canisters see your public principal ID for authorization (wallet, minting, admin where allowed). We do not receive your legal name, email, or date of birth from Internet Identity through this Site.
  • Age confirmation (local only). Before sign-in we ask you to confirm you are 13 or older. That choice is stored in your browser’s localStorage as trp_age_ok. We do not collect or store your date of birth.
  • On-chain data you create. Text prompts, generated images, credit purchases, and NFT mints are processed on the Internet Computer and recorded according to canister logic. On-chain data is public to the network and may be permanent.
  • Privacy notice dismissal. If shown, dismissal of our lightweight privacy bar is remembered in localStorage (trp_privacy_notice_dismissed).

What we do not do

  • No mailing lists, newsletters, or marketing email collection on this Site.
  • No third-party analytics (e.g. Google Analytics), no session replay (e.g. Hotjar, FullStory, LogRocket, Microsoft Clarity), and no advertising trackers.
  • No tracking cookies for analytics or advertising. We do not use a consent-management platform because we do not deploy non-essential trackers.

Third-party services

Internet Identity (identity.ic0.app or configured provider) authenticates you under DFINITY’s terms and privacy practices.

esm.sh — The Site loads pinned @dfinity/agent, @dfinity/auth-client, and @dfinity/candid (v2.4.1) from esm.sh so your browser can sign calls. That is functional infrastructure, not analytics. esm.sh may log standard CDN request metadata (IP address, user agent) under its own policies.

ICP API / boundary nodes — Canister calls and HTTP requests to the Internet Computer are routed through the public network.

Fonts

Typography is self-hosted from this Site’s asset canister. We do not load fonts from Google Fonts or other third-party font CDNs.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13. You must confirm you are 13 or older before Internet Identity sign-in. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Site, contact us at raven.icp@gmail.com.

Your choices

  • Clear site data in your browser to reset age confirmation and notice dismissal.
  • Use Internet Identity’s controls to manage anchors and sessions.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time by posting a revised version on the Site. The “Last updated” date above reflects the current version.

Contact

Privacy questions: raven.icp@gmail.com.
